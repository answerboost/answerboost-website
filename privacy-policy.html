Section 3

SMS / Text Messaging & Caller Data

The AnswerBoost platform sends automated SMS text messages to individuals who have voluntarily called our clients' business phone numbers and did not receive an answer. By initiating an inbound call to a client's business number, the caller has implicitly requested contact from that business. The first automated message identifies the business by name and provides immediate opt-out instructions.

Mobile Number Data — Non-Sharing Statement

All text messaging originator opt-in data and consent — including mobile phone numbers processed through the AnswerBoost missed call recovery service — is not shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This data is used solely to facilitate the missed call recovery service on behalf of our business clients.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on inbound call activity. Reply STOP to opt out of all messages at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. Opt-out requests are processed immediately and permanently by phone number. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Caller SMS data is retained for up to 12 months for service monitoring and is never sold to third parties.
