1. Who We Are

Geminus Holdings LLC doing business as AnswerBoost ("AnswerBoost," "we," "us," or "our") operates getanswerboost.com and provides automated missed call recovery services. We are headquartered in Irving, Texas.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@getanswerboost.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and business name when you submit a form or book a demo
  • Business information — company name, industry, and service configuration provided during onboarding
  • SMS opt-in data — phone number and consent when you subscribe to SMS communications from AnswerBoost

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, and IP address via cookies and analytics tools
  • SMS platform data — when end-consumers interact with our clients' automated SMS systems, we process phone numbers, message content, and timestamps on behalf of the client business

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and improve our missed call recovery automation services
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send service-related communications and updates
  • To send SMS updates you have opted into
  • To comply with legal obligations and carrier requirements
  • To analyze usage and improve our platform

We do not sell your personal information.

4. SMS and Text Messaging

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with any third parties.

AnswerBoost operates automated SMS systems on behalf of our business clients. End-consumers who receive SMS messages through our platform have called the client business first — their inbound call constitutes consent to receive a single follow-up text message.

Every SMS sent through our platform includes opt-out instructions. Recipients may reply STOP at any time to immediately unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

If you have opted in to receive SMS communications directly from AnswerBoost, you may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message or contacting us at hello@getanswerboost.com.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — including Telnyx (SMS delivery), Make.com (automation), and OpenAI (AI responses), who process data under confidentiality agreements solely to deliver our services
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the safety and rights of AnswerBoost or its users
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice provided before your data is transferred

6. Cookies

We use cookies to analyze traffic and improve our website. Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality. Our cookie banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications — unsubscribe at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@getanswerboost.com. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA). California residents may have additional rights under CCPA.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to business professionals and are not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify active clients. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
