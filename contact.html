SMS Messaging Program — How It Works

AnswerBoost provides automated SMS messaging services on behalf of local service businesses. Our system only sends text messages to individuals who have initiated contact first by calling the business. This is an inbound-initiated messaging program, not outbound cold messaging.

How a customer receives a message:

1. A customer calls a business that uses AnswerBoost (e.g. a water restoration company).
2. The call goes unanswered because the technician is on a job site.
3. Within 60 seconds, AnswerBoost automatically sends ONE SMS to the caller's number.
4. The customer can reply to get help, book an appointment, or reply STOP to opt out.

Sample message sent to callers:

"Hi! This is Dallas Restoration Pro. Sorry we missed your call — we are available 24/7 for water and fire damage emergencies. A team member will call you back shortly. Reply STOP to opt out."

Maximum message frequency: Up to 3 messages per missed call event — one initial recovery message, one AI response if the customer replies, and one follow-up after 2 hours if no response received. No further messages are sent after that.

Opt-out: Customers can reply STOP, QUIT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or END at any time to be immediately and permanently removed. A confirmation message is sent upon opt-out.
