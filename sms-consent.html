SMS Consumer Disclosure

AnswerBoost SMS

This page explains how and why you may receive an automated text message after calling a local business — and how to stop messages at any time.

How It Works
1

You call a local business

You place a call to a local service business — such as a restoration company, roofer, or contractor — that uses the AnswerBoost platform.

2

The call goes unanswered

If the business is unavailable, AnswerBoost detects the missed call automatically within seconds.

3

You receive one automated SMS

A single text message is sent to your number on the business's behalf, identifying the business by name and offering a way to connect or book a callback.

4

You stay in control

Every message includes opt-out instructions. Reply STOP at any time — you will never receive another message from that business.

Consent & Authorization
How Consent Is Collected

AnswerBoost operates as a B2B SMS platform for local service businesses. Each business that uses AnswerBoost signs up at getanswerboost.com and explicitly accepts our Terms of Service, which authorize AnswerBoost to send automated SMS follow-up messages to their missed callers on the business's behalf.

The business owner provides authorization at sign-up for their specific phone number and account. By accepting these terms, the business confirms they will only use AnswerBoost for inbound missed call recovery — contacting people who have already initiated contact by calling the business first.

No cold outbound messages are ever sent. Every SMS is triggered exclusively by an inbound missed call from the recipient's own phone number to the business's registered number.

What the Message Looks Like

Example of the first automated SMS you may receive after a missed call:

Example Message
Hi! You recently missed a call from [Business Name]. They'd love to connect — book a time here: getanswerboost.com/book. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply.

[Business Name] is always replaced with the actual business name — e.g., "Dallas Restoration Pro" or "North Texas Roofing."
