Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Who We Are

Geminus Holdings LLC doing business as AnswerBoost ("AnswerBoost," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website getanswerboost.com and provides automated missed call recovery services to local businesses. We are headquartered in Dallas, Texas.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and business name when you submit a form or book a demo
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, and IP address collected automatically via cookies and analytics
  • SMS data — when end-consumers interact with our clients' automated SMS systems, we process phone numbers, message content, and timestamps for the purpose of delivering the service
  • Business information — company name, industry, and service configuration provided by our clients during onboarding

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and improve our missed call recovery automation services
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send service-related communications and updates
  • To comply with legal obligations and carrier requirements
  • To analyze usage and improve our platform

4. SMS and Text Messaging

AnswerBoost operates automated SMS systems on behalf of our business clients. End-consumers who receive SMS messages through our platform have called the client business first — their inbound call constitutes consent to receive a single follow-up text message.

Every SMS sent through our platform includes opt-out instructions. Recipients may reply STOP at any time to immediately unsubscribe from all future messages. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers — including Telnyx (SMS delivery), Make.com (automation), and OpenAI (AI responses) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to analyze traffic and improve our website. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly. We use Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. SMS opt-out records are retained permanently to honor unsubscribe requests.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at [email protected]. California residents may have additional rights under CCPA.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no electronic transmission is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes.
