How it works

Set it once. Reviews roll in.

Your tech submits a quick job form. Everything else is automatic.

1

Tech submits job form

After completing a job, your tech fills out a 30-second Google Form — customer name, email, and service type.

2

AI writes the email

Our AI generates a personalized review request that references the actual job — not a generic template.

3

You approve or auto-send

Preview the email before it goes out, or enable auto-send with a 2-hour cancel window. You stay in control.

4

Customer gets routed smart

4–5 stars → Google review page. 1–3 stars → private feedback form. FTC compliant. Zero review gating.
